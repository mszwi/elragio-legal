Monkey Punch – Privacy Policy

Monkey Punch ("the game") is a single-player arcade game for Android and the web. This policy explains what the game stores, what Google's services in the Android game collect, and why.

What the game stores

  • Your best distance, your sound on/off setting and a count of runs since the last ad. These are saved only on your device (or in your browser) and are never sent to us.

Ads (Android)

The Android game shows a full-screen ad from Google AdMob between runs, about once every ten runs. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), and information about ad interactions and app performance. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first through Google's consent form; you can change your choice at any time with the AD PRIVACY button on the title screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Global leaderboard (Android)

If you use Google Play Games, the game sends your distance at the end of each run to Google Play Games so it can appear on the global "Furthest flight" leaderboard with your Play Games profile name and avatar. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; you control your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app.

What we do not collect

  • We run no servers and receive no data about you: no account, name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • The web version has no ads, leaderboard, analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its app data or your browser's site data, deletes everything it stored.
  • Leaderboard scores and your Play Games profile can be managed or deleted in the Google Play Games app.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page before the game starts collecting anything new.
