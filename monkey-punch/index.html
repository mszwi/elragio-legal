Monkey Punch – Privacy Policy

Monkey Punch ("the game") is a single-player arcade game for Android and the web. This policy explains what the game stores and why.

What the game stores

  • Your best distance and your sound on/off setting. These are saved only on your device (or in your browser) and are never sent to us.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking.
  • The Android game does not request internet access.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its app data or your browser's site data, deletes everything it stored.
  • Questions: email matthewszwinto@gmail.com.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page before the game starts collecting anything new, for example if ads or online leaderboards are added.
