Elragio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Elragio ("the game") is a single-player mobile game. This policy explains what data the game and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or photos. Your game progress (XP, upgrades, outfits, high scores) is stored only on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your scores and achievements are also sent to Google Play Games (see below).

The game uses the following third-party services, which collect data automatically:

ServicePurposeData
Google Firebase AnalyticsUnderstanding how the game is played (e.g. which screens are used, how long rounds last, where players stop playing) so we can improve it.App-instance ID, device model, OS version, country/region (derived from IP, not stored as precise location), app version, in-game events such as rounds played and upgrades bought.
Google Firebase CrashlyticsFinding and fixing crashes and bugs.Crash reports, error messages, device model, OS version, app state at the time of the crash, installation ID.
Google Play Games ServicesSigning you in automatically, world leaderboards and achievements.Your Play Games player ID and profile (gamer name and avatar, as set in Play Games), scores and achievement progress. Managed in the Play Games app or your Google account.
Google Play BillingProcessing optional in-app purchases (unlocking cities).Purchase records handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.
Google AdMobShowing ads, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch.Advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interactions. Ads may be personalised only if you consent where required.

Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and your choices

  • If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.
  • To request deletion of analytics data associated with your app-instance ID, contact us at the address below.

Deleting your Elragio data

  1. Game progress is stored only on your device: uninstalling Elragio deletes it.
  2. To delete analytics and crash data linked to your device, email matthewszwinto@gmail.com with the subject "Elragio data deletion". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Nothing is kept afterwards.
  3. Play Games scores and achievements belong to your Google account: delete them in the Play Games app (Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data).
  4. To reset your Advertising ID, go to Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Children

The game contains cartoon violence and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data retention and security

Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
