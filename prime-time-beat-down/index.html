Prime Time Beat Down – Privacy Policy

Prime Time Beat Down ("the game") is an online multiplayer game. This policy explains what the game collects and why.

What the game sends to our server

  • A random player id created on your device, so the server can recognise you when you reconnect.
  • The display name you choose, your character's look and your chosen Stalker kit, so other players can see you.
  • Your position and movements during a show, so other players see you move.

The server keeps your player id, name, how many times you have been the runner and any prizes you won (for example "ad-free"), so they carry over between seasons. Nothing else is stored.

What we do not collect

  • No account, email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking.
  • No chat.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google Play tells the game which products you own.

Deleting your data

  • Your game progress is stored on your device: uninstalling the game deletes it.
  • To delete the name and prizes kept on our server, email matthewszwinto@gmail.com with your display name.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if what the game collects changes.
